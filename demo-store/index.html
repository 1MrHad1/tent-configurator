Configured price: open the designer

What happens when you click "Add to cart" in the designer
  1. The configurator (an iframe on another origin) gets a signed quote from the pricing API.
  2. It saves the design, renders previews and attaches a production PDF.
  3. It posts add-to-cart to this page with the variant id and line item properties.
  4. This page's embed script calls /cart/add.js (mocked here) and replies with the result.
  5. The cart verifies the quote signature with POST /api/quote/verify, as an order webhook would.
